## Background

I work at the intersection of identity infrastructure and AI-native workflows — specifically: authentication, device intelligence, and identity verification in consumer fintech. The problems I spend most time on are fraud prevention vs. user friction, compliance as a competitive moat, and what good PM judgment looks like when you're delegating to agents instead of writing every artifact yourself.

This repo is where I think out loud, ship small things, and build the muscle of working in public.
